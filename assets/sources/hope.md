# HOPE objects

These assets are made from the object models of the [HOPE dataset](https://github.com/swtyree/hope-dataset)
(Household Objects for Pose Estimation, Tyree et al., NVIDIA), released under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and downloaded from the
[BOP benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/hope). The models were rescaled from
millimetres to metres and given collision hulls and placement regions.
