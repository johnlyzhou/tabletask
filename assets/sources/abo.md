# Amazon Berkeley Objects

These assets are made from [Amazon Berkeley Objects](https://amazon-berkeley-objects.s3.amazonaws.com/index.html)
3D models by Amazon.com, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the dataset was built by
Matthieu Guillaumin, Thomas Dideriksen, Kenan Deng, Himanshu Arora (Amazon.com), Jasmine Collins and
Jitendra Malik (UC Berkeley). Their parts were merged into one mesh with one base-colour texture, and they
were given collision hulls and placement regions.
