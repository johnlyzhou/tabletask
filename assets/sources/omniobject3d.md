# OmniObject3D

These assets are made from [OmniObject3D](https://omniobject3d.github.io/) raw scans
(Wu et al., CVPR 2023), [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
They were decimated, rescaled from millimetres, given collision hulls and placement
regions, and their textures were downsampled.
