# HANDAL objects

These assets are made from the artist-made object meshes of the [HANDAL dataset](https://nvlabs.github.io/HANDAL/)
(Guo et al., NVIDIA), released under [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/)
(non-commercial use only) and downloaded from the [BOP benchmark](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/handal).
The models were rescaled from millimetres to metres, laid flat in their resting pose, and given collision hulls
and placement regions.
