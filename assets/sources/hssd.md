# HSSD objects

These assets are made from the object models of the [Habitat Synthetic Scenes Dataset (HSSD)](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hssd/hssd-models)
by Khanna et al., released under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/)
(non-commercial use only), downloaded from [hssd/hssd-hab](https://huggingface.co/datasets/hssd/hssd-hab).
Their textures were decoded from Basis Universal to PNG and baked into one texture per object; the
meshes were given collision hulls and placement regions.
