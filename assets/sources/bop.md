# BOP benchmark objects

These assets are made from object models published with the [BOP benchmark](https://bop.felk.cvut.cz/datasets/)
and downloaded from its Hugging Face mirror. Each keeps the license of its dataset:

- **HOT3D** ([models](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/hot3d)): Meta HOT3D object models: CC BY-SA 4.0, and they may not be sold or built into a product that is sold.
- **Toyota Light (TYO-L)** ([models](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/tyol)): CC BY-NC 4.0.
- **HomebrewedDB (HB)** ([models](https://huggingface.co/datasets/bop-benchmark/hb)): CC0 1.0.

The models were rescaled from millimetres to metres, turned upright where needed, given one texture
(HomebrewedDB vertex colours were baked into a texture), and given collision hulls and placement regions.
